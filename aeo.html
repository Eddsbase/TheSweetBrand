What is AEO?

Answer engine optimization (AEO) is the work of helping AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI answers accurately understand, mention and recommend your business.

SEO is about ranking a page. AEO is about being part of the answer. AI tools build answers from what's written about you across the web: your own site, reviews, press, directories and community discussions. If that picture is thin or inconsistent, they may name your competitors instead.

No one can guarantee what an AI tool will say. What we can do is measure where you stand today and improve the signals these tools rely on.

FAQ

Questions

Is AEO replacing SEO?

No. Strong SEO is still a foundation, since many AI tools pull from search results. AEO adds a focus on being clearly described and mentioned across the sources AI tools read.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend us?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. AI answers change and vary by question. We measure, improve the inputs, and report honestly.

What's in the free snapshot?

A short look at how several AI assistants answer a set of buyer questions in your category, and whether your brand or your competitors appear.

Do you need access to our accounts?

Not for the snapshot. It only uses public AI tools and your public website.
