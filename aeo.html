SEO vs. AEO

Ranking a page is not the same as being the answer.

SEO

Wins a spot in the list of links. The goal is the click.

AEO

Wins a mention inside the answer itself. The goal is to be named, described accurately and recommended, often before anyone clicks.

SEO is still the foundation. Many AI tools search the web before they answer, so a site they can't find or read won't get cited. AEO builds on that base.

What moves the needle

How AI tools decide who to mention

Findable, readable pages

AI crawlers need access, and your key content should load as plain HTML that search engines have indexed.

Clear answers

Pages that answer the real questions buyers ask, in plain language near the top, are easier to quote.

Credible outside sources

AI answers lean heavily on what others say about you: press, industry lists, community discussions and reviews.

Consistent facts

Your name, what you do, where you serve and who you serve should match everywhere, from your site to directories and profiles.

Reviews and coverage

For "best" and "who should I use" questions, review sites and third party write-ups carry real weight.

Each platform is different

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google cite different sources, so we measure each one separately.

FAQ

Questions

Isn't this just SEO?

SEO is the foundation, and we keep it. AEO adds work most SEO plans skip: outside mentions, question-level tracking and measuring each AI platform separately.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend us?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. AI answers change from one run to the next and from one platform to another. We measure against a baseline, improve what these tools rely on, and report honestly.

Can't we just buy a tracking tool?

A tool shows you the gap. Closing it takes content, PR and reviews, which is hands-on work.

What about llms.txt and other quick fixes?

Some are cheap hygiene and we'll handle them, but we won't sell them as the main event. We tell you which tactics have real support and which don't.

What's in the free snapshot?

A look at how several AI assistants answer a set of buyer questions in your category, and whether your brand or your competitors appear. It only uses public AI tools and your public website, so no account access is needed.
