Privacy Policy

This page explains what information The Sweet Brand handles through its websites, thesweetbrand.co and aeo.thesweetbrand.co, and how we use it. It describes our current practices as plainly as we can.

Information you send us

The main way we receive personal information is when you email us at info@thesweetbrand.co. We receive whatever you choose to include, such as your name, email address, company, website and the details of your request.

The request form on the AEO page is turned off. The contact form on thesweetbrand.co is not connected to a form service that we use to receive messages, so please email us to make sure your message reaches us. If we connect a form in the future, we will update this page.

How we use it

We use the information you send to reply to you, discuss and provide the services you ask about, and keep a record of our conversation.

Who else may handle it

We use outside providers to run these sites and our email, and they process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • thesweetbrand.co is hosted on GitHub Pages.
  • aeo.thesweetbrand.co is hosted on Vercel.
  • Our email provider handles messages sent to info@thesweetbrand.co.

Hosting providers typically record technical information when you visit a site, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and operations. We may also share information if the law requires it.

Cookies, analytics and tracking

At the time of this update, the code of these sites does not include analytics or advertising integrations, and the fonts, images and videos on these pages are served from our own sites. Our hosting providers may use their own technologies as described in their policies. If we add analytics or advertising tools, we will update this page.

Links to other sites

Our sites link to other websites, including sweetsuite.co and sweetdata.co. Those sites have their own privacy notices, and this page does not cover them.

Your choices and requests

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing info@thesweetbrand.co. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable law. We may need to keep some information where the law requires it.

Changes

We may update this page as our sites or practices change. The date below shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this page can go to info@thesweetbrand.co.
